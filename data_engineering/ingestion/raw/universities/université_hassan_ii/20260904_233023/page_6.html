Top Research Honors for UH2C

Hassan II University of Casablanca

Today, Hassan II University of Casablanca continues to play a central role in the development of higher education in Morocco, offering a wide range of training programs and actively contributing to scientific research and innovation. It also aims to train citizens capable of understanding the complexities of the current world and facing them with discernment, preparing them to address global challenges and societal issues.
